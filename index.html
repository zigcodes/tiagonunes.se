    All people are mosaics

    And I am no exception.

    Even when in the surface a person looks unidimensional and simple to understand, I do believe everyone has many pieces that make them who they are.

    These pieces can, sometimes, be less visible or completely hidden.

    Strangely enough these pieces might even seems contradictory, and not belonging together in the same mind, but once again that is how humans are humans, by carrying paradoxes and antitheses in themselves in (in)perfect balance.

    For that, I will not worry in curating this blog too much to be about "one theme" or to serve any other purpose than being just some pieces of my mosaic.

    Enjoy!
